## Project-authored records

- `data/model-sync-v9.5.0.json`: recovered decisions, numeric targets, assumptions, pending checks.
- `data/chart-references.json`: stored range anchors and DPM projections.
- `reference/archive/forum-p3-final-source.json`: recovered frozen reference tables.
- Gear/formula archives, Master/potion/class-audit CSVs, and WDEF sweep JSON: historical packages and project fixtures/audits.

Original XLSX workbooks are not redistributed. Historical filename references may still identify omitted artifacts; they do not mean those files are available here.

## Attribution still to complete

Original formulas/equipment need titles/authors, public links, assumptions used, retrieval dates, and precision. Forum records need snapshot dates, inclusion/exclusion rules, original posts, and MW normalization. Nine classes have stored Top10 median/#1 records; Extreme is limited to DK/NL/Paladin. DPM uses frozen-curve piecewise interpolation/extrapolation and is not measured player damage.

`retrievedOn` describes project source recovery, not necessarily a leaderboard snapshot date. Missing authors, links, dates, and attribution are not inferred.
